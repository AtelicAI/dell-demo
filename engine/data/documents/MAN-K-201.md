# MAN-K-201: Compressor rod packing and vent excerpt

Origin: synthetic example; operational_approval: false; version: 1.

Tag K-201, single-stage gas compressor between P-102 (suction, about 7 bar(a)) and P-201 (discharge, about 12.6 bar(a)), nominal throughput 1800 kg/h. Rod packing leakage is routed to a packing vent; a rising vent flow is an expected wear indicator and should be trended against the OEM allowable range.

Packing vent emissions are a distinct source from discharge-side valve or flange leaks. When methane is detected downwind of the discharge area, compare against compressor-adjacent sensors before attributing it to the compressor. Excerpt values are illustrative and not an approved OEM manual.
