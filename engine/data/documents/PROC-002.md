# PROC-002: Isolation and work planning review

Status: draft, not yet approved for operations (version 2).

This is a planning checklist, not permission to isolate equipment. An authorized site engineer must identify the actual equipment and upstream/downstream connectivity from approved drawings, assess the local hazard conditions, identify applicable permits and review the proposed work order. Drawn connectivity and a draft procedure are insufficient to authorize field execution. This system provides no plant-control interface.
