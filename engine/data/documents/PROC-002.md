# PROC-002: Isolation and work planning review

Origin: synthetic example; operational_approval: false; version: 2.

This is a planning checklist, not permission to isolate equipment. An authorized site engineer must identify the actual equipment and upstream/downstream connectivity from approved drawings, assess the local hazard conditions, identify applicable permits and review the proposed work order. Synthetic connectivity and an example procedure are insufficient to authorize field execution. This demonstration provides no plant-control interface.
