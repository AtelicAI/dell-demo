# PROC-001: Observation and source verification

Origin: synthetic example; operational_approval: false; version: 2.

Record the incident and asset identifiers, camera/annotation provenance, observation time and unresolved alternatives. Review independent methane and wind sensor quality flags and timestamps. Check whether the camera-to-equipment mapping is calibrated or illustrative. A drifting plume alone does not establish the leaking fitting. Separate hypotheses from recorded observations. Escalate missing or contradictory evidence to the reviewing engineer.
