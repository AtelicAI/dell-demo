# DS-XV-2104: Discharge isolation valve datasheet

Tag XV-2104, discharge isolation valve on line L-201 between P-201 and F-205, with a normally closed branch to XV-2105. Synthetic design: 4-inch PN40 trunnion ball valve, bolted bonnet, braided graphite stem packing with live-loaded gland follower. Design gas service: natural gas, operating about 12.5 bar(a) and 303 K downstream of the aftercooler.

Potential external emission points: stem packing, bonnet joint, body/end flange joints. Packing leakage can be intermittent and load-dependent, increasing during pressure or temperature changes. Example gland follower torque: 35 N·m per nut, tightened evenly in two passes. A soap test is a spot check and does not replace a repeat optical gas imaging survey.
