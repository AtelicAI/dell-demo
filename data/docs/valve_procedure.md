# MP-XV-101: Inlet isolation valve stem leak inspection

## Scope
Inspection of XV-101 (inlet isolation valve) and the P-100 inlet line after a suspected methane release at the valve.

## Steps
1. Confirm the release with a handheld gas detector or OGI camera at a safe distance.
2. Check the XV-101 stem packing and gland follower for gas at the stem.
3. Check the XV-101 body-to-bonnet joint and both end flanges.
4. Record readings and photos. Attach them to the work order.

## Permits
- Hot-work permit is not required for visual inspection.
- Upstream isolation at the pipeline ESD valve is needed before any packing work on XV-101.
