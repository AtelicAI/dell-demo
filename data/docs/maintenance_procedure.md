# MP-VENT-01: Vent assembly leak inspection (illustrative)

> Illustrative procedure for a representative skid. Not an approved site operating instruction.

## Scope
Inspection of VENT-01 (vertical release assembly) and the P-102 vent header after a suspected methane release.

## Steps
1. Confirm the release with a handheld gas detector or OGI camera at a safe distance.
2. Check the vent-base flange and gasket (previous leak point, WO-2025-0412).
3. Check whether PSV-102 or BDV-103 is passing gas into P-102.
4. Record readings and photos. Attach them to the work order.

## Permits
- Hot-work permit is not required for visual inspection.
- Gas test and confined-space check required before any isolation work.
